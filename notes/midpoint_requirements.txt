Mid-Point Video
---------------
Just record your agent doing something and talk through it.  If you have a more
research oriented project then present some preliminary results and explain
them!  The video should be about 3 minutes.

At a high level your video should be no more than 10 minutes (certainly can be
shorter) and we want you to just show us your agent working.  If it's more of a
research project you can make a video briefly presenting your results.

Question:
For research-oriented projects that don’t have a working prototype, what should
the midpoint demo video show? Would a walkthrough of our experimental pipeline
and preliminary results, like baseline numbers and early plots, meet the
“working prototype” expectation? And should the README cover reproducing those
results instead of running a system?

Answer:
Exactly yes!  The video should demo the experimental setting and one key result
you've collected so far!  Same with the README.  Should walk through how to
reproduce the results.  Great question
